Frequently asked questions

What our clients ask us.

Which documents should I prepare?

Usually: ID, proof of income, recent bank statements and, for a purchase, the sales agreement or reservation contract. We send you the full list for your profile after our first conversation.

I live abroad: can I do everything remotely?

Yes. We build and follow your file remotely, by WhatsApp, email or video call, and tell you in advance which steps require your signature.

How long does it take to get an answer?

It depends on the bank and on how complete the file is. We give you a realistic timeline at the assessment stage and keep you informed at every step.

Do you work with businesses and developers?

Yes: investment loans, leasing, cash flow, developer loans, debt restructuring and fundraising mandates.
